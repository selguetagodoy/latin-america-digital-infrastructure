# Data center market presence

This module keeps data-center coverage at market level rather than publishing a granular facility inventory.

Verified public registry observations as of 2026-08-13 show the following initial operator presence:

| Country | Market | Operators observed in public interconnection registries |
|---|---|---|
| Argentina | Buenos Aires | Cirion |
| Brazil | Sao Paulo | Equinix, Ascenty, Scala Data Centers |
| Chile | Santiago | Equinix, Ascenty |
| Chile | Valparaiso | Scala Data Centers |
| Colombia | Bogota | Equinix, ODATA |
| Mexico | Queretaro | Equinix, KIO |
| Mexico | Mexico City | KIO |
| Peru | Lima | Equinix, Cirion, GTD |

## Interpretation

Presence in this table means that at least one facility associated with the operator is represented in the PeeringDB facility registry for the market. It is not an exhaustive inventory and should not be interpreted as a facility count, capacity estimate or market-share measure.

The repository deliberately avoids publishing street-level facility locations or inferring power capacity, investment values, customers or operational characteristics that are not required for regional comparison.

Source basis: PeeringDB facility registry and operator records, verified 2026-08-13.
