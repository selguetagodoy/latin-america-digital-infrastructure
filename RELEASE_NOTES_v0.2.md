# Latin America Digital Infrastructure — v0.2.0

Release date: 13 August 2026

## Scope

This public release consolidates the comparative observatory of digital infrastructure across eight Latin American benchmark markets: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico, Panama and Peru.

## Included public assets

- Regional benchmark dataset with 27 comparable variables.
- Cloud-region inventory.
- Internet exchange point inventory.
- Selected submarine-connectivity inventory.
- Operator-country presence dataset.
- Country profiles.
- Methodology and data dictionary.
- Public observatory website.
- Reproducibility scripts and automated checks.

## Methodological principles

- Missing values remain missing.
- Operational, planned and announced infrastructure are kept separate.
- Harmonization is used only when source definitions are sufficiently comparable.
- Public and primary evidence is preferred.
- Third-party source data remain subject to the terms of their original publishers.
